# Global Rules

These rules apply across the entire Static network, including every server, gamemode, in-game chat, and official
community space. Gamemode-specific rules apply in addition to these rules.

Rules may be updated at any time. Players are responsible for staying up to date and following staff instructions.

## Spamming

Do not repeatedly send the same or similar messages, commands, characters, or words. Messages intended to flood chat,
interrupt conversations, or otherwise disrupt communication are also considered spam.

## Excessive Characters

Do not use excessive repeated characters, symbols, emojis, capitalization, or other formatting that clutters chat or
makes it difficult to read.

## Encouraging Chat Spam

Do not encourage, request, organize, or help other players spam chat. You may be punished for participating even if you
do not personally send the spam.

## Excessive Toxicity

Do not repeatedly act in a hostile, insulting, disrespectful, or excessively negative manner that creates a toxic
environment. Friendly banter is allowed, but it must not become harassment or disrupt the community.

## Inappropriate Behavior

Behavior that is excessively disruptive, offensive, disrespectful, sexual, or otherwise unsuitable for the community
is prohibited. This includes impersonating staff, posting NSFW content, and sharing malicious or unsafe links. Staff may
act against behavior that harms the community even when it is not specifically covered by another rule.

## Hate Speech

Slurs, hateful language, and content that attacks, degrades, or promotes hatred toward an individual or group based on
protected characteristics are prohibited.

## Racism and Encouraging Racism

Racist remarks, slurs, stereotypes, discrimination, and encouraging others to participate in racist behavior are
strictly prohibited.

## Encouraging Self-Harm

Do not encourage, promote, glorify, or tell another person to harm or kill themselves, including as a joke.

## Leaking Personal Information

Do not share, request, threaten to share, or attempt to obtain another person's private information without permission.
This includes home addresses, phone numbers, passwords, IP addresses, and other sensitive or identifying information.

## Punishment Evasion

Do not attempt to avoid or bypass a punishment by using alternate accounts, account sharing, name changes, VPNs, or
any other method. Accounts used to evade a punishment may also be punished.

## Real-World Trading

Do not buy, sell, trade, or exchange in-game items, currency, accounts, ranks, services, or other server-related assets
for real-world money, gift cards, goods, or services unless explicitly authorized by Static.

## DDoS, Doxxing, and Real-World Threats

Threatening, encouraging, coordinating, or claiming to have carried out a DDoS attack, doxxing, or another real-world
attack against a player or the server is prohibited.

## Advertising

Do not advertise or promote other servers, communities, websites, social media accounts, products, or services without
staff permission.

## Hacked Clients

Do not use unauthorized Minecraft clients, modifications, scripts, or other software that provides an unfair advantage
or bypasses intended game mechanics.

## Nuker

Do not use a hacked client, modification, script, or other software to automatically or rapidly break multiple blocks
beyond what is possible through normal gameplay.

## Alt Grinding

Do not use alternate accounts to unfairly gain rewards, currency, items, progression, votes, event rewards, or other
benefits intended to be earned by individual players.

## Inappropriate IGN or Skin

Minecraft usernames, skins, capes, and other visible account identities must not be sexual, hateful, discriminatory,
offensive, or otherwise inappropriate.

## Exploiting Bugs or Glitches

Do not abuse unintended server or game bugs, glitches, exploits, or mechanics to gain an unfair advantage, duplicate
items, bypass restrictions, or negatively affect the server or other players. Report bugs and exploits to staff instead
of abusing or sharing them.

## Scamming

Scamming and other deceptive behavior intended to take items, currency, or advantages from another player are not
allowed. Gamemode-specific insiding rules may provide additional protections.

## Language Policy

Use English in public communication channels so that staff can moderate effectively and players can receive support.

## Staff Authority and Enforcement

Staff decisions regarding rule interpretation and enforcement are final. Staff may act against harmful behavior that is
not explicitly listed, and players must comply with staff instructions.
