# Seasonal Rewards

Seasonal rewards recognize the players and teams who finish a season at the top of their gamemode's competitive leaderboard. Depending on the gamemode and placement, rewards may include store gift cards and exclusive seasonal tags.

Final standings are recorded when a season ends. Players must follow the applicable server and gamemode rules to remain eligible; boosting or otherwise creating an unfair competitive advantage may result in disqualification.

Reward amounts, qualification details, and delivery methods vary by gamemode. Choose a gamemode below to view its current rewards:
