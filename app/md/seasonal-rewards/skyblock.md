# Skyblock Seasonal Rewards

At the end of each Skyblock Season, the five highest-ranked islands on Island Top will receive seasonal rewards.

## How to qualify

- Finish the season in one of the top five Island Top positions. Island Top is ranked by island value.
- Follow the [Global Rules](/rules) and [Skyblock Rules](/rules/skyblock). Boosting or otherwise creating an unfair
  competitive advantage may disqualify an island from receiving rewards.
- Placement is determined when the season ends. The members of each winning island at that time will receive the
  island's placement tag.

## Island Top rewards

| Place | Store gift card | Tag       |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| #1    | $250            | S2 #1 Tag |
| #2    | $100            | S2 #2 Tag |
| #3    | $50             | S2 #3 Tag |
| #4    | None            | S2 #4 Tag |
| #5    | None            | S2 #5 Tag |

## How rewards are delivered

The store gift card for a winning island will always be given to the island owner. The owner may choose how to
distribute it among the island's members. See the [Gift Cards wiki guide](/wiki/misc/gift-cards) for information about
checking a GC balance, paying another player, withdrawing a gift card, and using it in the store.

Every member of a winning island will receive the tag for that island's final placement.
