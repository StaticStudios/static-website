# Skyblock Monthly & Seasonal Rewards

Competitive islands earn leaderboard points by placing in the weekly Island Leaderboard. Those points determine both
monthly winners and the season-long Island Top standings. Read the
[Island Leaderboards wiki guide](/wiki/skyblock/leaderboards) for a complete explanation of scoring and resets.

## How to qualify

- Play on a Competitive island. Casual islands are not eligible for leaderboard rewards.
- For monthly rewards, finish the month in one of the top three positions by monthly points.
- For seasonal rewards, finish the season in one of the top five positions by season points.
- Follow the [Global Rules](/rules) and [Skyblock Rules](/rules/skyblock). Boosting or otherwise creating an unfair
  competitive advantage may disqualify an island from receiving rewards.
- Monthly placement is finalized at the monthly reset. Seasonal placement is finalized when the Skyblock season ends.

| Place | Store gift card |
|-------|-----------------|

Monthly points return to zero after the winners are finalized. Season points and leaderboard history are not erased by
the monthly reset.

## How rewards are delivered

After results are finalized, a member of the winning island must open a ticket in the [Static Discord](/discord) to
claim the reward. The island owner's Minecraft account must be linked to the owner's Discord account.

The store gift card for a winning island is given to the island owner. The owner may choose how to distribute it among
the island's members. See the [Gift Cards wiki guide](/wiki/misc/gift-cards) for information about checking a GC
balance, paying another player, withdrawing a gift card, and using it in the store.

Every member of a season-winning island will receive the chat tag for that island's final placement.
