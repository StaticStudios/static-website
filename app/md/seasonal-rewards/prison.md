# Prison Seasonal Rewards

At the end of each Prison Season, the five highest-ranked gangs on Gang Top will receive seasonal rewards.

## How to qualify

- Finish the season in one of the top five Gang Top positions.
- Follow the [Global Rules](/rules) and [Prison Rules](/rules/prison). Boosting or otherwise creating an unfair
  competitive advantage may disqualify a player from receiving rewards.
- Placement is determined by each gangs's points when the season ends.

## Gang Top rewards

| Place | Store gift card | Tag       |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| #1    | $200            | S1 #1 Tag |
| #2    | $100            | S1 #2 Tag |
| #3    | $50             | S1 #3 Tag |
| #4    | None            | S1 #4 Tag |
| #5    | None            | S1 #5 Tag |

## How rewards are delivered

The store gift card for a winning gang will always be given to the gang owner. The owner may choose how to
distribute it among the gang's members. See the [Gift Cards wiki guide](/wiki/misc/gift-cards) for information about
checking a GC balance, paying another player, withdrawing a gift card, and using it in the store.

Every member of a winning gang will receive the tag for that gang's final placement.
