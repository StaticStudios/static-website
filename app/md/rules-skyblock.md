# Skyblock Rules

These rules apply specifically to the Skyblock gamemode and are enforced alongside the [Global Rules](/rules).

## Alt Islands

Your alternate accounts may not join or participate in an island other than your primary island. Do not bring an alt to
an island created by another player to benefit from that island's resources, upgrades, or other advantages.

## Auto Clicking

AFK auto clicking is allowed only on your island. Auto clicking while participating in PvP within `/warzone` is
prohibited.

## Macros

Except for the on-island AFK auto clicking expressly allowed above, macros, scripts, and other forms of automatic
farming that perform actions without active player input are prohibited.

## Account Limit

No more than five of your accounts may be connected to the server at the same time. If you exceed this limit, every one
of your connected accounts may be punished.

## Island Rotation

Do not repeatedly remove and re-add island members to bypass the island member limit for the purpose of gaining an unfair
advantage on island leaderboards.

For example, kicking a member when they log off and inviting another player to use that otherwise occupied member slot
is considered island rotation.

Abusing island rotation may result in disqualification from any applicable leaderboards.

## Island Boosting

Do not funnel items, currency, resources, labor, or other benefits to a competitive island for the purpose of helping it
earn or improve a position on `/is top`.

## Insiding

Do not steal from, deliberately grief, or damage an island. Leaving the island afterward does not make these actions
acceptable. Anything placed on or contributed to an island becomes the island's property and may be removed only with
permission from the island owner. When leaving, you may take only personal items kept in your inventory or `/pv`.

## Reverse Insiding

Do not remove a player from your island without a valid reason for the purpose of taking items or resources they
contributed to the island.
