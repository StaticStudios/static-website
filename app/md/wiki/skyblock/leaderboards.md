# Skyblock Island Leaderboards

The Island Leaderboard is a competition for **competitive islands** based on how much island value they gain. It uses
short weekly races to award points, monthly standings to award gift cards, and season-long standings to award the
largest prizes and exclusive chat tags.

Use `/island top` or `/istop` in game to open the leaderboard.

> **Summary:** grow your island value during each week, finish in the weekly top five to earn points, and
> collect as many points as possible before the monthly and seasonal standings close.

---

## Who can compete?

Only islands created as **Competitive** are eligible. Casual islands can still gain island value, but they never appear
in the competitive leaderboard or earn leaderboard points. A competitive island can permanently switch to Casual, but
a Casual island cannot switch to Competitive.

All participating islands must follow the [Global Rules](/rules) and [Skyblock Rules](/rules/skyblock). Boosting or
otherwise gaining an unfair advantage can result in disqualification.

---

## What is being ranked?

The weekly leaderboard ranks islands by **net island value gained during the current week**, not by their all-time
island value. An established island and a newer island therefore compete on the progress they make during the same
period.

The value an island has at the beginning of a week acts as its starting point. Only growth above that starting point
counts toward the weekly leaderboard.

---

## Weekly competition and points

During a week, the in-game menu shows the islands with the largest value gain. Placements remain live until the
reset, so an island can move up or down as competitors gain value. The points shown as “points to be earned” are a
preview of what the island will receive if it keeps that position through the end of the week.

| Weekly place | Points awarded |
|--------------|----------------|
| #1           | 5 points       |
| #2           | 4 points       |
| #3           | 3 points       |
| #4           | 2 points       |
| #5           | 1 point        |

When the weekly reset happens, those placements become final and the points are added to both the island's monthly and
season totals. A new weekly value-gain race then begins from zero. **A weekly reset does not remove points already
earned during the month or season.**

A normal leaderboard week follows a Sunday-through-Saturday schedule. The countdown above shows the exact reset date
and time using your local settings. Month boundaries take priority:

- If a new month starts in the middle of a week, the previous month's final week ends and a new week starts immediately
  for the new month. That opening week still ends after Saturday.
- If a month ends before Saturday, the current week ends early with the month so its final points can be included in
  that month's results.

---

## Monthly competition and reset

Monthly standings add together every weekly point earned during that calendar month. Weekly points do not carry into
the next month. If islands finish with the same number of monthly points, their total eligible value gain for the month
is used as the tiebreaker.

When the next leaderboard month begins, the final weekly period is closed, the monthly winners are locked in, and every
island's monthly points return to zero. This is the **monthly reset**. Weekly history and season points are not erased.

The monthly top three receive gift-card rewards. See the
[Skyblock Monthly & Seasonal Rewards page](/seasonal-rewards/skyblock) for the current prizes and claiming instructions.

---

## Season standings

Season points keep accumulating after monthly resets. Every point awarded from a finalized weekly leaderboard counts
toward the island's season total. The season leaderboard therefore rewards islands that perform consistently across
many weeks and months, rather than only during one strong month.

If islands have the same number of season points, their eligible value gain across the season is used as the
tiebreaker. At the end of the Skyblock season, the top five islands receive the configured seasonal rewards. Season
points reset only when a new Skyblock season begins.

---

## Claiming rewards

Leaderboard rewards are claimed by opening a ticket in the [Static Discord](/discord) after the results are finalized.
The claimant must be the owner the winning island, and their Minecraft account must be linked to the
their Discord account. Store gift cards are issued to the island owner, who may distribute the value among island
members.

For current monthly and seasonal prize amounts, placement tags, and delivery details, visit the
[Skyblock Monthly & Seasonal Rewards page](/seasonal-rewards/skyblock).
