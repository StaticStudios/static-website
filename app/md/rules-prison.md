# Prison Rules

These rules apply specifically to the Prison gamemode and are enforced alongside the [Global Rules](/rules).

## Gang Rotation

Do not repeatedly remove and replace gang members to work around the gang member limit or gain an advantage over other
gangs on the leaderboard.

## Account Limit

No more than five of your accounts may be connected to the server at the same time. If you exceed this limit, every one
of your connected accounts may be punished.

## Macros

Macros, scripts, and other forms of automated gameplay are prohibited.

## Boosting

Players, gangs, and other teams may not boost others in ways that create an unfair competitive advantage. Boosting for
seasonal rewards is prohibited and may disqualify every involved player from receiving rewards.
